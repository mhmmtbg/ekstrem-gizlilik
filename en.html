Ekstrem Privacy Policy (English)

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Ekstrem reads credit card statement PDFs on the phone and helps plan spending.

  • Ekstrem does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. It has no account, server, ads, analytics or third-party tracking.
  • Statement transactions, incomes, notes, budgets and plans you enter are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. PDF files and statement passwords are not stored. Pasted bank messages are read once to fill a form and are not stored; the app does not access SMS.
  • Backups, images and CSV files are created only when you ask (plus an automatic on-device backup) in the phone's Documents › Ekstrem folder. Sharing them is always started by you.
  • Android may back up app data to your Google account if Android Backup is enabled on the device; this is a Google service governed by Google's privacy policy.
  • Permissions: Internet (required by the WebView; no data is sent), notifications (optional payment reminders), boot/wake lock (keep reminders after restart), biometrics (optional app lock), storage on Android 10 and below (saving files), Google Play Billing (Ekstrem Pro purchase).
  • Ekstrem Pro is a one-time purchase sold through Google Play Billing. Payment details are processed by Google under Google's privacy policy; Ekstrem never sees your card, name or e-mail. The app only asks Google Play on the phone whether this account owns Ekstrem Pro; nothing is sent to the developer or any server. The trial start date is stored only on the phone.
  • Delete all data with Settings › Backup › Delete all data, or by uninstalling the app.
  • The app is intended for users aged 18+. Contact: the developer e-mail address shown on Google Play.
